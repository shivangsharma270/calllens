How the priority score is computed

Frequency

Calls with this root cause ÷ all analysed calls.

Severity

Average weight of its calls ÷ 3. A call weighs 3 if Fatal, 2 if Non-Fatal but the buyer was frustrated or the call did not reach its goal, 1 otherwise.

Priority = frequency × severity × 100

Ranges 0–100. A cause scores 100 only if every analysed call has it and all of them are Fatal.
